Your first world

  1. Choose a sharp photo with clear foreground subjects.
  2. Sign in, add credits, then choose Create this memory.
  3. Enter the finished world and open WOMetrics for its generation facts and available measurements.

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, iPhone HEIC and short videos are supported. AI infers the surfaces outside your photograph.

One key. Your image. 3D.

Use Node 22.18+ and your own AI3D key. No dependency installation required. Add AI3D_API_KEY to .env in the extracted folder, then replace the image path:

tar -xzf wompar-demo.tar.gz
cd wompar-demo
node demo/wompar/wompar.mjs --provider ai3d \
  --source "/absolute/path/to/my-photo.jpg"
